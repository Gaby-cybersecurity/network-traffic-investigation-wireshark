SOC PROJECT 2 — IOC REVIEW

Purpose
-------
Review artifacts identified during the investigation and distinguish
confirmed indicators from investigation artifacts.

-------------------

8. Decoded Command
   SOC Project 2 - Suspicious PowerShell Test

   Assessment:
   Controlled laboratory test output. Not considered malicious content.

Conclusion
----------
The investigation identified a suspicious PowerShell execution pattern,
primarily the use of -EncodedCommand.

No confirmed malicious IOC was established from the available evidence.

The SHA256 hash and process information should nevertheless be preserved
as investigation artifacts for future comparison or enrichment.

Overall Finding
---------------
Suspicious PowerShell behavior was successfully detected, extracted,
decoded, and investigated in a controlled laboratory environment.

The evidence demonstrates the investigation workflow without establishing
that the activity was malicious.
